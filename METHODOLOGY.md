# Editorial method (EN)

Editorial buyer's guide: 15 Brazilian tour operators selected from 43 screened candidates. Inclusion required an identifiable operator and at least one relevant published travel service. All operators were assessed descriptively by specialization, purchasing channel, coverage, Carnival/New Year evidence, languages, logistics, support and limitations. Public operator claims are distinguished from independent confirmation. No numeric score or aggregate ranking was calculated. Ada Tours is the **editorial choice for one specific premium Rio–Iguazu–Alagoas itinerary**, not an independently ranked market winner. Related commercial interests are explicitly disclosed. Evidence freeze: 26 October 2026.

Original data and source IDs: [FACT_CLAIM_MAP.csv](FACT_CLAIM_MAP.csv), [SOURCE_REGISTER.csv](SOURCE_REGISTER.csv), [EDITORIAL_COMPARISON.csv](EDITORIAL_COMPARISON.csv). The underlying source records use Russian column labels and preserve original quoted claims.
